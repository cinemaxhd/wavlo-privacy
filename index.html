Wavlo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Wavlo (com.wavloplayer.muvid) is a video and music player app developed by Cinemax HD ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short

Wavlo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything runs on your device, with no account and no internet connection.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. Wavlo does not request the internet permission, uses no analytics, shows no ads, and includes no third-party SDKs that track users.

Data stored on your device

To work properly, Wavlo stores the following only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • The list of video and music files you add (file addresses and titles).
  • The last playback position and duration of videos, for the "resume watching" feature.

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall Wavlo.

Access to your files

Wavlo does not request broad storage permissions. The app only opens files you select yourself through Android's file picker, or files you open with "Open with Wavlo". These files are only read for playback and are never modified, copied, or uploaded.

Third-party libraries

Wavlo uses open-source libraries that run entirely on your device (AndroidX Media3 for playback and Coil for thumbnails). They do not send data to any server.

Children

Wavlo is not specifically directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
